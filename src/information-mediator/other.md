## 10. Other Resources

### 10.1 Related specifications
This specification should be read together with:

- `govstack-cfr-2.1.0`;
- GovStack Common Terminology;
- GovStack Architecture Specification 2.1.0; and
- relevant GovStack Digital Identity, Workflow, Registry, and security-related specifications.

### 10.2 Implementation guidance

Country- or region-specific legal, governance, operational, or procurement
guidance SHOULD be published in separate implementation guides and SHALL NOT
override immutable requirements in this specification.

### 10.3 Future considerations
Future revisions MAY further standardize:

- federation exchange patterns;
- event contract governance;
- policy-as-code integration;
- conformance profiles for centralized and federated deployment modes; and
- reference API sets for directory, federation, and audit retrieval.
